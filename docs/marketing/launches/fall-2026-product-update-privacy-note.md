# Fall 2026 product update: privacy note (draft)

A section for the October-November product update sent to community admins (the first
issue of the monthly newsletter, E002, covering everything since the S1/S2 campaign).
Operator decision 2026-11-24: no separate message to admins about the policy change; this
note in the update is the "more prominent notice" the Privacy Policy promises.

Source of truth for every number: `/privacy/` (Data Retention) as of November 24, 2026,
and the announcement post `/blog/privacy-policy-data-retention-update/`. Publish the site
first: the link must resolve before this goes out.

The link is wrapped (`<...>`) in the Discord version so it does not take the message's
one link preview from the update's lead item. Unwrap it only if this note is the lead.

---

## Discord version

**🔒 Privacy: how long Subo keeps data**
Subo now cleans up after communities that stopped using it. Your community's data is deleted after **180 days** without activity once Subo is removed from your server, or after **24 months** while Subo is still installed. You get a heads-up 30, 7 and 1 days before, and any activity keeps everything. Paid plans are never affected.
As the server owner, you can also delete your community's data yourself in **Settings**, under **Danger Zone**, with 7 days to change your mind.
We also fixed how our policy described **Anonymous** projects. Nothing changes for you: nobody in your community, you included, can see who answered what. Subo keeps the link to itself, so members can still delete their own answers.
What changed: <https://subo.gg/blog/privacy-policy-data-retention-update/>

## Notes for whoever assembles the update

- **Screen labels:** "Settings" and "Danger Zone" are what the web app shows (the card is
  on the General tab of Settings, and only the Discord server owner can use it).
- **Translations:** the update is sent in each admin's language. These lines are new copy:
  translate them with the rest of the issue (subo-localization rules; FR settings word is
  "Paramètres", ES "Ajustes", "quiet/inactive" ruling applies).
- **Do not promise more than the policy.** Avoid "Subo can't see anonymous answers" or
  "no one, not even Subo": Subo keeps the link (decision D9).
