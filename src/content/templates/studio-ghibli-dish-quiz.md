Food in Studio Ghibli films looks better than food in real life, and everyone has a favorite. This quiz tells each member which one they are.

## Comfort habits, not trivia

Nobody needs to remember the films. The six questions ask how you unwind and look after people, and the answers point to the meal that matches: a dramatic breakfast, a joyful bowl of ramen, quiet comfort, a gift made by hand, a lunch packed for everyone or toast eaten on the way to an adventure.
