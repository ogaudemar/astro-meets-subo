Ask a server "which Pokémon are you?" and you get forty opinions. Post this quiz and everyone gets an answer, plus the scores to compare.

## Six questions, six partners

The questions cover your morning, your kind of adventure, your battle strategy, what friends count on you for, your favorite weather and your biggest weakness. Every option scores two Pokémon: 3 points to one, 1 to another, rotated so every Pokémon can reach the same top score.

## Make it your own

The structure works for any six results. Swap in the starters from your favorite generation, your server's mascots or the classes in your own game, keep the 3-and-1 scoring on each option, and the reveal and scores still work.
