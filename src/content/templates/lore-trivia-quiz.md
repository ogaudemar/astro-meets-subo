Whenever you have a body of knowledge and want to find out (or reward) who actually knows it, this quiz grades every answer, teaches as it goes, and acts on the result automatically.

Use it for game lore and canon, a new patch's mechanics, your product's docs, server rules, a study set, or onboarding material. Members answer, Subo tells them right or wrong on the spot after each question, and anyone who clears the pass bar earns an achievement they can't get any other way.

## Better than a plain quiz bot

A typical quiz bot just counts answers. Subo grades them, teaches through the per-answer feedback, and then acts: the achievement is granted automatically, in the same flow, with no mod checking a spreadsheet. Write the "wrong answer" feedback to explain the correct answer and every miss becomes a teaching moment instead of a dead end.

The example runs five questions on The Legend of Zelda and passes at four out of five. Change the subject, the questions, the pass bar, and the reward to fit your community.

## Variants

- **Certification or onboarding quiz:** the same quiz over your product docs or policy, with a "Certified" achievement for anyone who passes.
- **Server-rules gate:** three to five questions on the rules, with a "Verified" achievement that unlocks the server through a linked Discord role.
- **Harder questions worth more:** weight tougher questions higher and reward XP proportional to the total.
- **Study set with retakes:** allow unlimited attempts so members can drill until they pass.
