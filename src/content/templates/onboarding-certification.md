Nobody reads the pinned rules, and nobody remembers a wall-of-text onboarding doc. A short quiz with instant feedback fixes both: it makes the material *stick*, and it turns "I read the rules" into a **visible, earned credential** — granted automatically the moment someone passes.

Every answer gives immediate right-or-wrong feedback with the correct answer, so even a failed attempt teaches. Clear the pass threshold and Subo grants a Certified badge on the spot, with no mod checking a spreadsheet. Teaching and gatekeeping in the same sixty seconds.

## Knowledge that unlocks status

Pair the Certified badge with whatever it should open — an advanced role, a members-only channel, a verified tier. Because it's an achievement, not a raw role, it survives being cloned into any server and still links to a local role once you wire it up. The example runs as a community onboarding and rules certification; swap the questions and it becomes product certification, partner training, or employee onboarding.

## Variants

- **Product certification:** swap in product-knowledge questions and rename the badge.
- **Pre/post training assessment:** clone twice, before and after training, and compare scores.
- **Retakes allowed:** let people study and try again.
- **Distinction tier:** add a second badge for a perfect score above the standard pass.
