Some people skip the opening. This quiz is for everyone else.

## Ten songs, ten shows

Each question names a famous theme and its artist: LiSA's *Gurenge*, TK's *Unravel*, Linked Horizon's *Guren no Yumiya*, YOASOBI's *Idol*, the Seatbelts' *Tank!* and five more, including two endings. Members pick the show from four options and find out right away if they're right.

At the end, skip logic reads the score and shows one of four titles, from "Skip-intro button" under 5 to "Opening connoisseur" for a perfect 10.
