"What are we playing tonight?" can eat half an hour of a game night. This template turns it into a one-tap poll where every option is a real game with its icon, and the live tally ends the debate before anyone has to open voice chat.

## Games as options

Type **@** in an option and pick under **Games**, and the option becomes that game: its name and icon show in the poll, on the web, in the Results tab and in the exports. The template starts with five co-op and party staples. Keep the ones your community plays and swap the rest; the [walkthrough](/recipes/people-games-and-times-in-options/) shows how.

Game options don't depend on your server, so they survive a clone. Copy the poll to another community and the games come with it.

## Variants

- **What to stream next:** let your audience pick the next game for the channel.
- **Tournament game:** choose the title for the next community tournament.
- **Backlog vote:** list the games everyone owns and never plays, and let the vote pick one.
- **Pick the night too:** run the [Time Poll](/templates/party-like-1999-time-poll/) alongside it to settle when as well as what.
