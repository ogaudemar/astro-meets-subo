The "which is best" poll is a fast engagement hit that also tells you what your audience actually likes. This template is the polished single-question version: pick one favorite from a short list, watch the live tally, and change your mind if the debate shifts.

Run it for the fun favorites vote: favorite character, weapon, map, class, genre, snack, whatever your community has opinions about.

## One tap, real signal

A poll is a single question, and here it is a short list with a meaningful emoji on every option. Members pick their favorite and see which one is winning. Beyond a bit of fun, the result is useful input for content and events.

The example is a favorite game-genre vote, gamer-relevant and obviously swap-me. Change the options to your community's world.

## Variants

- **Character or map vote:** swap the options for your game's roster.
- **Product favorite:** ask which feature or plan people like best.
- **Bracket seed:** run several favorites polls and ladder the winners into a bracket.
- **Recurring:** run it monthly to see how favorites shift over time.
