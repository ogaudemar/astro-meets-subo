"Do You Love Your Mom and Her Two-Hit Multi-Target Attacks?" is a real light novel. So is "I've Been Killing Slimes for 300 Years and Maxed Out My Level." That's what makes this quiz hard.

## Six rounds, one fake each

Each round shows four titles. Three are published light novels, and one we wrote ourselves. Members pick the one they think is fake and find out right away. A wrong guess quotes their pick back to them and reveals the real fake.

At the end, skip logic reads the score and shows one of three results, from "Lost in another world" to "Light novel scholar."
