Most Minecraft quizzes ask what a Creeper does. This one asks which mob the Creeper was supposed to be.

## Ten questions, four pickaxes

The questions cover the game's history (its first name, the coding mistake behind the Creeper, the sound of the Ghast) and its mechanics (the smallest working Nether portal, Eyes of Ender, the length of a day, what pistons can't push). Members find out right away whether they got each one, and why.

At the end, skip logic reads the score and shows one result: a wooden pickaxe under 5, iron for 5 to 7, diamond for 8 or 9, and netherite for a perfect 10.
