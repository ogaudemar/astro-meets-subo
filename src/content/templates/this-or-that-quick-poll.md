The two-option "this or that" is the single most common poll people run, and this template is the polished version of it: one tap, a live tally, and the freedom to change your vote. It earns its place over a plain Discord reaction poll on presentation, a unique emoji per option, a topic image, and a running results bar, not on complexity.

Run it as the daily or weekly quick poll: a two-way debate, an A/B call, a "which do you prefer."

## One question, done well

A poll is a single question, and here the prompt is the whole poll. Members tap a side and watch the bar move. For a binding decision use the governance vote; for a discussion use the debate poll with its defend-your-pick follow-up; for pure fast fun, this.

The example is a cats-versus-dogs debate, universally recognizable and obviously swap-me.

## Variants

- **Daily poll:** run a fresh matchup every day to keep the channel active.
- **A/B feedback:** two design options, two names, two directions, and let the room decide.
- **Gamer versus:** two characters, two weapons, two maps.
- **Anonymous:** switch to anonymous when the topic is a little spicier.
