A community that spans time zones can't agree on "8pm" because 8pm means something different for everyone. This template is a one-question poll where each option is a date and time, and every member reads it in their own time zone, in Discord and on the web.

The example is a countdown party on New Year's Eve 1999. It's a joke with a purpose: change the dates before you post, and the template shows you how.

## Times as options

Type **@** in an option, pick **Date & time**, and choose the date and time. You enter it in your own time zone, and Subo shows it in each reader's zone and language, in the poll, on the web, in the Results tab and in the exports. The [walkthrough](/recipes/people-games-and-times-in-options/) covers the details.

On Discord, a button label can't change with the reader's time zone, so the poll uses the **Emoji only** answer style: the buttons are numbered and the times are listed in the poll above them.

## Variants

- **Raid night:** offer three evenings and let the guild pick.
- **Stream schedule:** ask your audience which slot they can actually watch.
- **AMA or community call:** find the hour that suits the most members across regions.
- **Contest deadline:** let entrants vote on when submissions close.
