Recognition works best when the community does the choosing. This template is a one-question poll where the options are your members themselves: each one shows a name and avatar, so voters know exactly who they're picking, in Discord and on the web.

Run it once a month for your Member of the Month, or any time you want the community to pick a person: an MVP, a helper, a best build.

## People as options

Type **@** in an option and pick under **Members**, and the option becomes that member, with their avatar, everywhere the poll appears, including the Results tab and the exports. The template ships with four placeholders because a template can't carry your members; swapping them takes a minute. The [walkthrough](/recipes/people-games-and-times-in-options/) shows each step.

Results stay hidden until the vote closes. A visible tally turns a people vote into a popularity snowball, so the reveal waits for the end.

## Variants

- **MVP of the tournament:** nominate the standout players after an event.
- **Helper of the month:** let members thank the people who answer questions in support channels.
- **Best clip or build of the week:** nominate the creators and let the community pick.
- **Team vote:** pick under **Roles** instead of **Members** to vote between teams or squads.
