Every K-pop fan has an answer to "which member are you?" This quiz gives your whole server one, plus the scores to argue about in chat.

## Six positions, six questions

The questions cover the group chat, a day off, karaoke, the five minutes before a show, a dream comeback concept and what fans would know you for. Every option scores two positions: 3 points to one, 1 to another. The pairs rotate so all six positions can reach the same top score, which means the result comes from the member's answers and not from how the questions were written.

## Why positions and not real idols

A quiz built around one group only works in that group's fan server. Positions work in all of them, from a server for one group to a general K-pop community. If your server is about a single group, the reveals take a minute to rename.
