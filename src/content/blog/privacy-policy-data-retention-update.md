We updated the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) and the [Terms of Service](/terms/) on November 24, 2026. Most of the change describes data retention rules that now run in Subo every night. Part of it corrects things the previous version got wrong.

## What Subo deletes, and when

- **Inactive communities.** A community's surveys, responses, XP and settings are deleted after 180 days without activity once Subo has been removed from its server, or after 24 months without activity while Subo is still installed. Admins get a notice 30, 7 and 1 days before, in Discord or by email. Opening the community in the web app, creating a survey or getting new answers keeps everything. Communities on a paid plan are never deleted for inactivity.
- **Deletion on request.** A server owner can delete the community's data from the Settings page of the web app. It happens 7 days later, so there is time to cancel, and Subo then leaves the server.
- **Member data.** Your membership and XP in a community are deleted 12 months after you leave it. If you don't use Subo for 3 years, your identity is removed and your answers stay with the communities, without anything that identifies you.
- **Logs and backups.** Activity logs and the records of messages we send are kept 24 months. Encrypted backups are kept 7 days.

The full list, with every data type, is in the [Data Retention](/privacy/#data-retention) section of the policy.

## A correction about Full Anonymous

The previous policy said Subo keeps no link between an anonymous answer and the person who gave it. That was not accurate. Subo keeps that link, so you can delete your own answers, a survey can count one response per person, and XP rewards still work. It is never shown to anyone in the community: the survey's creator and the server's admins see answers without names, and exports contain no Discord IDs. What members see in Discord and in the web app has always worked this way; only the policy described it wrongly.

## Your rights, in one place

Your account page at [app.subo.gg/app/account](https://app.subo.gg/app/account) lets you review, export and delete the answers you gave in each community, anonymous ones included, or delete your whole Subo account. In Discord, `/profile` takes you there.

Questions about any of this: [hello@subo.ai](mailto:hello@subo.ai) or the [support server](/support/).
