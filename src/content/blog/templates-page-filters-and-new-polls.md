The [Templates page](https://app.subo.gg/app/templates) in the web app got easier to browse, and the catalog got three new polls built on the [@ options](/blog/discord-poll-images-people-games-dates/) from last week.

## Find a template faster

- **Use-case chips.** One row across the top: New, Polls, Quizzes, Feedback, Applications, Events, Onboarding, Research and Just for fun.
- **A Features filter.** Looking for an example of skip logic, answer piping, calculated fields, or members, games and times in options? Pick the feature and you see only the templates that use it.
- **Tag suggestions.** Search suggests tags as you type, and the tags on a template link back to the gallery filtered on that tag.
- **On a phone,** the filters sit behind one Filters button instead of taking the whole screen.

We also cleaned up the tags, so each idea has one tag instead of three spellings of it.

## Three new polls

Each is one question, and each option is picked with **@** in the editor:

- [🏅 Member of the Month](/templates/member-of-the-month-poll/): the options are members, with their names and avatars. Results stay hidden until the vote closes.
- [🎮 Game Night: What Are We Playing?](/templates/game-night-poll/): five real games with their icons, and a live tally.
- [🪩 Party Like It's 1999 (Time Poll)](/templates/party-like-1999-time-poll/): time slots that each member reads in their own time zone. The example slots are New Year's Eve 1999, so change them before you post.

The recipe [Vote on people, games and times](/recipes/people-games-and-times-in-options/) explains how each kind of option works and how to swap in your own.

## Older templates, newer features

[File uploads](/blog/discord-file-uploads/) are now in three templates: [Bug Report](/templates/bug-report-form/) takes up to 3 screenshots, [Beta Tester Recruitment](/templates/playtester-beta-recruitment/) a resume, portfolio or past feedback, and [Staff Application](/templates/staff-application/) a resume or screenshots of mod work. All three are optional.

Seventeen templates now use [optional questions](/blog/discord-survey-optional-questions/): follow-ups like "anything else?" say "(optional)" and have a Skip button. In Staff Application, "anything else" is now a single optional question instead of a Yes/No and a follow-up.

## Also fixed

- Copying a project to another community now keeps a member's or role's name in an option. It used to leave the option blank.
- Template pages in the app show games, members and times by name everywhere, including the questions list and the invite preview, instead of raw codes.
