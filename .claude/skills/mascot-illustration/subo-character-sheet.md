# Subo Character Canon (immutable)

Everything here should **never drift** between illustrations. When a generation
gets any of this wrong, fix it in Photopea rather than shipping it.

> Confidence note: some exact values (proportions, hex codes) were lost when the
> source chat truncated. Fields marked **[VERIFY]** should be pinned down from a
> reference render and updated here. Treat the rest as canon.

## Body
- Rounded, soft "blob" robot. Glossy plastic / soft rubber feel, rounded edges,
  **no sharp corners**.
- Body color: bright lime green. #9eff00
- Pink emissive outline / border on the character: #e1287e

## Head
- Topped with 3 **bar charts** (the signature that reads "survey bot").
- Default bar-chart color scheme uses, from left to right: purple (#745399), grey (#848ca4), aqua (#00f4ff) with pink outlines (#e1287e) 
  their bar charts to signal a copy (e.g. the teleported clone uses all-purple
  bars with pink outlines).

## Eyes
- **Left eye:** a square with a pink border and a pink inner square.
- **Right eye:** normally the checkbox / checkmark motif ("winking" Subo)
- **Exception:** when Subo is depicted as "wide eyed", both eyes
  become like the left-eye (square-in-square) style.
- **Never** generate half-finished, mismatched-by-accident, or malformed eyes.

## Mouth
- Present and expressive (cheerful). Neutral expression is a simple straight line. **Never** omit the mouth. 

## Hands & feet
- Simple, complete, cartoon hands and feet (2 hands, 2 feet)
- Hands are the #1 thing the model breaks, especially on small background copies. Inspect every one.

## Supporting cast: the Discord "blob"
- Represents the user / community member (styled after the Discord blob emoji).
- **Always reacting**: wonder, excitement, discovery, a learning moment.
- Second in the focal hierarchy (after Subo).

## Character acting (how they behave on screen)
- **Subo is always active**: presenting, pressing, painting, teleporting,
  handing over, announcing, building. A host, never a spectator.
- **The blob is always the audience**: it receives, reacts, and has the "aha!"
  Subo enables; the blob discovers.

## Never-do list
- No malformed / half-finished eyes.
- No missing mouth.
- No merged or duplicated limbs; no hands fused to furniture.
- No sharp corners on the character.
- No invented text/names on or near the character.
- Don't recolor Subo's body per scene; only **clone bar charts** change color,
  and only to mark a clone.
