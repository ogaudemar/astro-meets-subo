# Example: The Poll Toolkit

- **Feature:** Polls vs full product (hero for "Discord native polls vs Subo").
- **Metaphor:** The toolkit / kit-of-tools.
- **Premise:** A native Discord poll is one plain tool. Subo is the whole kit: the
  poll plus everything around it (anonymity, cloning, surveys/forms, rewards). Subo
  proudly opens the case; the blob discovers there's more than a vote. Honest, not a
  swipe at a named rival (the "plain poll" is Discord's own native feature, not a
  competitor bot).

## Brief
Subo as a warm workshop host proudly flipping open a glowing, soft-metallic toolkit
on a workbench, gesturing into it, presenting toward camera.

- **The kit (the joke):** neat glowing slots hold a small, clearly-different set of
  tools. Keep to **four**, well-separated and readable:
  1. a **poll** tool: a small horizontal bar-chart card (glowing brightest, the one
     just used), echoing Subo's bar-chart head,
  2. a small **padlock** (anonymous voting),
  3. a little **clipboard / form card** (surveys & forms),
  4. a small **trophy or medal** (rewards / XP).
- **The comparison gag:** on the workbench beside the open case sits a plain, small,
  un-glowing native Discord poll card. Basic next to the kit. This is the head-to-head,
  stated visually and honestly.
- **Foreground:** a Discord blob leans in from the side, wide-eyed and delighted,
  discovering the kit holds more than a vote.
- **Tone:** warm, playful, workshop coziness.
- **Ratio:** 2:1.

## Notes
- **Icons, not text** on the tools; the render invents gibberish type otherwise (see
  the poll-desk cleanup pain). Read the tools by shape alone. The one intentional label
  is a small **"NATIVE POLL"** tag on the plain comparison card (it helps the gag read).
- **Neon sign: "GO PRO."** The workshop's neon tagline sells the casual-to-pro upgrade.
  Keep it to this line. Do **not** use "MORE THAN JUST A VOTE" or any "more than
  just X" phrasing: that construction is banned by the `blog-writing` skill as an AI
  tell, and the first render used it.
- Distinct from the **poll desk** (`poll-desk`, best-poll-bots hero): that's a TV
  broadcast results show about *how we vote*; this is a workbench kit about *how much
  more than a vote you get*. Don't reuse the desk/board.
- Winner/hero glow in **teal/cyan** (Subo's aqua). Purple only on Subo's head bar and
  an optional clone sparkle; keep it minimal.
- Four tools is the cap. If it reads busy, drop the trophy first and keep poll + lock +
  form (the three the article leans on hardest).
- Expect Photopea cleanup: inspect the blob's hands, kill any invented text, keep Subo
  a foot in front of the workbench so he doesn't merge into it.
