# Example: The Template Library

- **Feature:** The template library (ready-to-run, cloneable surveys/polls/
  quizzes).
- **Metaphor:** Library.
- **Premise:** Subo the curator hands a wide-eyed blob a glowing, ready-made
  survey card; the blob is having a "so THAT'S how you do it" moment. You don't
  start from a blank page.
- **Pairs with the blog post:** `subo-template-library-launch.md`.

## Brief
Subo as a cheerful library curator, mid-gesture, presenting a glowing, ready-made
survey "card" it just pulled off the shelf, handing it toward a wide-eyed Discord
member blob.

- **Subo:** lightly dressed as a curator/librarian (round glasses perched on, a
  small "Templates" name tag or curator's vest). Warm grin. One arm extended
  offering the card; the other resting on a rolling library ladder or display
  stand. Keep the mascot clearly recognizable, not over-costumed.
- **The offered card:** a mini-survey that is visibly alive (tiny emoji answer
  buttons, a small bar chart, a checkmark, a star rating), glowing at the edges.
  A clean **duplicate sparkles off it** (a copy peeling away in a shimmer) so it
  reads as "clone in one click." The blob already cradles its own freshly-cloned
  glowing copy.
- **Behind Subo:** a cozy-but-magical library wall with **three labeled, softly
  glowing shelf sections: CONNECT, DISCOVER, ORGANIZE**, each a distinct hue.
  Each holds a few template cards standing like framed books, hinting at a survey
  type (a trophy/leaderboard on Connect, rating faces or a magnifying glass on
  Discover, a checklist/calendar on Organize). A few per shelf only.
- **Small gag (subtle, optional):** a crumpled, totally blank sheet labeled "FROM
  SCRATCH" tossed aside; Subo isn't looking at it.
- **Tone:** warm, inviting, the joy of discovery.
- **Palette:** signature warm metallic + teal with pink highlights; each shelf
  section a subtly distinct hue so the three read at a glance.
- **Composition:** 2:1, uncluttered. Foreground = Subo + the cloning card + the
  inspired blob; the three labeled shelves read instantly in the background.

## Notes
- Reuses **clone = sparkle** and the **"FROM SCRATCH" old-way gag.**
- Environment: see the "Libraries" entry in
  [../recurring-metaphors.md](../recurring-metaphors.md).
