# Example: The Game Show Host

- **Feature:** Quizzes, grading, scoring, auto-rewards (XP / badges).
- **Metaphor:** Game show.
- **Premise:** Subo just revealed the final answer; the chaos of the old
  spreadsheet era is literally underfoot while everything behind him hums along
  automatically.

## Brief
Subo dressed as a vintage game-show MC (sparkly blazer, oversized microphone,
huge grin) standing center-stage on a neon-lit set, with the energy of someone
who just revealed the final answer.

- **Foreground:** three Discord-style member blobs at podiums with buzzers, each
  with a score counter ticking upward above their head. One has a "Squish" badge
  just popping onto its screen; another has a small "checkmark Correct!" burst
  flying off its question card.
- **Behind Subo:** a big split-screen scoreboard glowing on the wall. One panel
  shows the argmax wheel mid-spin landing on a segment name ("Blob"); another
  shows an XP bar filling automatically; another shows `[score_competitor]: 14`
  as a pill chip.
- **At Subo's feet:** a crumpled spreadsheet labeled "THE OLD WAY" with a broken
  calculator, visibly kicked aside (maybe mid-air from the kick). Subo isn't
  looking at it; he's looking at the camera.
- **Tone:** triumphant but playful.
- **Ratio:** 2:1.

## Notes
- Uses the "old way kicked aside" gag (spreadsheet + broken calculator).
- Subo looks at camera while automation runs behind him: the core "Subo enables"
  dynamic.
