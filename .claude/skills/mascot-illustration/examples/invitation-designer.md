# Example: The Invitation Designer / Craftsman

- **Feature:** Invitation customization ("you're sending an invitation, not a
  form").
- **Metaphor:** Painting workshop.
- **Premise:** Subo is dressing up the invitation by hand, so the invite looks
  designed, not default.

## Brief
Subo with a paintbrush and color palette, painting the border of a Discord embed
card that floats beside him, one edge actively being painted.

- **On the embed:** a custom thumbnail slot, a couple of emoji answer buttons, a
  little "i info" button.
- **Around the scene:** a few paint swatches / color chips scattered for the
  customization vibe.
- **Ratio:** 2:1, fixed.
- **Keep it simple:** do not overcrowd; the painted embed is the one clear focus.

## Notes
- Minimalist by design; a good reference for "2 to 3 elements, lots of breathing
  room."
