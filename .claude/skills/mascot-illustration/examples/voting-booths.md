# Example: The Voting Booths

- **Feature:** privacy modes (Transparent, Semi-Private, Anonymous): hero image for
  `/blog/complete-guide-anonymous-surveys-discord/`.
- **Metaphor:** three voting booths in a row, one per privacy mode.
- **Premise:** the post's spine is "who can see an individual member's answers," and its
  biggest section is the three modes. Three booths with less and less visibility explain
  that in one glance. Subo enables privacy by holding the curtain and **pointedly looking
  away**, which is the post's strongest line: no one can see, including you.
- **Why not a spy or detective scene:** trench coats and noir lighting belong to the
  alternative-series newsroom. Disguise glasses on the blob carry the "anonymous" joke
  without borrowing that register.

## Brief

```
SCENE
  A warm, cozy little polling hall: polished wood floor, soft rounded booths,
  pink and cyan accent light. Three voting booths in a row, left to right:
  (1) a fully transparent glass booth with a small glowing ballot card inside,
  (2) a booth with a curtain covering only its upper half,
  (3) a booth with a full, heavy velvet curtain in deep teal.
  Above each booth, a small round glowing icon sign, no words:
  an open eye, a half-closed eye, a fully closed eye.

MAIN ACTION
  Subo, a cheerful 3D mascot robot, stands beside the third booth, holding its
  curtain slightly open for the blob with one hand. With exaggerated politeness
  he turns his head away from the booth toward the camera, grinning and winking,
  the very picture of "I'm not looking." Subo's canon: a soft, rounded,
  glossy lime-green (#9eff00) body with pink (#e1287e) outlines, three bar
  charts on top of his head (purple, grey, aqua, left to right, pink outlines),
  a square-in-square left eye, a checkmark-in-box right eye, and a wide smile.

SUPPORTING CHARACTER
  A purple Discord-style blob pokes its head out of the full-curtain booth,
  wearing classic novelty disguise glasses (thick black frames, big fake nose,
  bushy mustache), beaming with delighted "nobody will ever know" excitement.

BACKGROUND
  Soft and blurred: a couple of warm pendant lamps and a plain wall with a
  subtle Discord-like logo glow. Plenty of empty breathing room.

PROPS
  The three booths (privacy modes), the disguise glasses, the curtain.
  Optional small gag: a tiny discarded "HELLO my name is" sticker on the floor,
  left blank.

LIGHTING
  Warm cinematic, soft key + rim, pink/cyan accents, slight bloom, shallow DoF.
  The anonymous booth gets the warmest, most inviting light.

PALETTE
  Warm wood + teal velvet + pink highlights. Purple only on the blob and the
  head bar.

COMPOSITION
  2:1 ratio. Eye level, slight wide angle. Booths recede left to right in gentle
  perspective; Subo and the third booth on the right two-thirds, dominant.
  Blob second, booths third, background last.

STYLE
  Pixar / WALL-E / modern high-end 3D animation.

NEGATIVE
  No photorealism, painterly, anime, or flat illustration.
  No words on the booths or signs (icons only). No invented names or extra text.
  No malformed hands/eyes. No missing mouth. No merged limbs. No clutter.
  Subo's hand must not fuse with the curtain.
```

## Notes

- **Icons, not words, on the booths.** The mode names are English product terms that get
  translated; eye icons survive every locale.
- **The half curtain is the weakest signal** of the three (Semi-Private means admins can
  see and members can't, which no curtain shows exactly). The eye icons carry it.
- **Deep teal is the privacy hue.** Reuse it for any later privacy-themed image.

## Final asset

`public/images/blog/anonymous-surveys/anonymous-voting-booth.webp` (2:1, 1200x600)

**Shipped 2026-10-21.** On brief: the three booths, eye icons (open / half / closed with
lashes), teal velvet, the blob in fake nose and mustache glasses peeking out, Subo holding
the curtain with no hand fusion. The blob also picked up a ballot card with a checkmark,
which helps. Booths sit flat in a row rather than receding in perspective; fine.

Subo came back winking with his resting straight-line mouth rather than the grin the brief
asked for. That works: the wink is the "I'm not looking" beat, and a deadpan face sells
the politeness better than a grin. The head bars came back correct (purple / grey / aqua).

## Alternative concept, rendered and parked: the masquerade door

Tried for the same post and not used, because the booths are funnier. The render exists
(`members-only.webp`, kept by the user outside the repo) and may suit a future
**verified-audience / members-only** image, since its point is **anonymous and
authenticated at the same time**: Subo checks you are a real member, then forgets who you
are.

```
SCENE
  The grand entrance of a cozy masquerade ball: tall double doors cracked open,
  warm golden light and soft confetti spilling out, a velvet rope on brass posts.
  A small elegant sign on the rope post reads "MEMBERS ONLY".

MAIN ACTION
  Subo is the doorman. He has just stamped the blob's glowing membership card with
  a big green checkmark (the card shows only a Discord-like logo and the checkmark,
  no name). With his other hand he presents the blob with a sparkly deep-teal
  masquerade mask, grinning. Beside him, the blob's name tag (blank lines, no name)
  is dissolving into little sparkles in midair: Subo already forgot who you are.
  [+ Subo canon paragraph; optional bow tie]

SUPPORTING CHARACTER
  A purple Discord-style blob, holding its stamped card, leans toward the mask
  with delighted wonder: "I'm in, and nobody knows it's me."

BACKGROUND
  Through the open doors, blurred: two or three masked blob silhouettes dancing
  under a warm chandelier.

PALETTE
  Warm gold + deep teal velvet + pink highlights.

NEGATIVE
  No text other than "MEMBERS ONLY". No names on the card or tag.
  Background dancers must not draw focus. [+ standard negatives]
```

**What came back:** on brief, including the stamp in Subo's hand (three props held without
breakage), the dissolving tag and the masked dancers. Subo winks with his resting
straight mouth, which suits a doorman who is in on the secret. "MEMBERS ONLY" rendered in a serif, which is English baked into the art; a
localized post would need it removed or swapped.
