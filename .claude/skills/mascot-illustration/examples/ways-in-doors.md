# Example: Two Doors, One Room (Ways in)

- **Feature:** Ways in (the Discord post, the web link, journeys and campaigns, each
  switched on separately; Audience Sources and verification on the web link). Hero
  image for the Ways in announcement post (staged at
  `docs/drafts/survey-twitch-youtube-discord-one-link.md`).
- **Metaphor:** a cozy clubhouse lounge (the project) with two doors on its back wall,
  each with its own big glowing on/off switch, both on.
- **Premise:** the post's spine is "one project, several independent ways in, every
  response lands in the same place." Two doors into one room, each with its own switch,
  show that at a glance: Discord members come through one door, people from Twitch,
  YouTube and Steam through the other, and they all end up in the same room. Subo is
  the host who just flipped the second switch on.
- **Why not the masquerade doorman** (parked in [voting-booths.md](voting-booths.md)):
  that image is about one door and one check (members only). Ways in is about
  *several* doors. Subo is not a gatekeeper here. He is the host opening another
  entrance. Verification only shows up as a small checkmark badge on each guest.
- **Why not the teleporter:** that one is about sending a project out (distribution).
  This one is about people coming in.

## Brief

```
SCENE
  A warm, cozy round clubhouse lounge seen from inside: soft rounded sofas and
  cushions, polished wood floor, warm lamps, pink and cyan accent light. On the
  back wall, two doors side by side, both wide open:
  (1) LEFT: a rounded wooden door with a small glowing speech-bubble icon above it,
      in Discord-like blurple.
  (2) RIGHT: a tall arched doorway glowing soft aqua, with a small glowing
      chain-link icon above it.
  Beside each door, a big chunky rounded rocker switch on the wall, both flipped
  ON and glowing lime green (#9eff00).

MAIN ACTION
  Subo stands in the middle of the room, between the two doors and slightly in
  front of them. One hand has just flipped the right door's switch on (fingers
  still near it, not touching or fused). The other arm sweeps wide in a big,
  welcoming host gesture toward the arched doorway: "come in, come in."
  Subo's canon: a soft, rounded, glossy lime-green (#9eff00) body with pink
  (#e1287e) emissive outlines, no sharp corners, three bar charts on top of his
  head (purple #745399, grey #848ca4, aqua #00f4ff, left to right, pink
  outlines), two square eyes each with a pink border and a pink inner square
  pupil, and a mouth.
  Subo's expression: open eyes (both square-in-square, no wink), wide proud grin.

SUPPORTING CHARACTER
  A purple Discord-style blob sits on a cozy cushion near the left door, having
  just come in that way. It turns toward the arched doorway with delighted
  wonder, mouth open, one hand raised in a friendly wave at the new arrivals:
  "they can come in too?"

  Coming through the arched doorway, three small round guest characters (simple
  soft blob shapes, the same family as the Discord blob but in neutral warm
  tones: cream, soft grey, pale teal). Each wears one small round badge on its
  chest, icon only, no letters:
  - a violet badge with a small heart (a follow),
  - a red badge with a white play triangle,
  - a dark navy badge with a small game controller.
  Each badge has a tiny lime-green checkmark in its corner (verified). The
  guests are smaller than the blob, mid-step, curious and happy.

BACKGROUND
  Soft and blurred: warm round windows with a hint of evening sky, one shelf
  with a couple of plants. Optional: a small brass mail slot low in the wall
  with a glowing envelope just sliding through (the third way in: journeys and
  campaigns), kept small and secondary. Plenty of empty breathing room.

PROPS
  The two open doors, the two lit switches, the guests' badges.

LIGHTING
  Warm cinematic, soft key + rim, pink/cyan accents, slight bloom, shallow DoF.
  The arched doorway is the brightest light source, spilling soft aqua light
  onto the floor toward Subo.

PALETTE
  Warm wood + soft cream interior + pink highlights + aqua doorway glow. Lime
  green only on Subo and the two switches. Purple only on the Discord blob,
  Subo's head bar and the violet badge. Red and navy only on their two badges.

COMPOSITION
  2:1 ratio. Eye level, slight wide angle. Both doors fully visible on the back
  wall, the arched one right of center. Subo dominant in the center foreground,
  the blob second (left foreground), the arriving guests third (right
  mid-ground, in the doorway), background last.

STYLE
  Pixar / WALL-E / modern high-end 3D animation.

NEGATIVE
  No photorealism, painterly, anime, or flat illustration.
  No text, letters or words anywhere: icons only on the signs and badges.
  No real platform logos (no Discord, Twitch, YouTube or Steam wordmarks or
  exact logos); simple generic icons only.
  No malformed hands/eyes. No missing mouth. No merged limbs. No clutter.
  Subo's hand must not fuse with the switch or the wall.
  The guests must not outshine the blob: keep them smaller and partly in the
  doorway.
```

## Notes

- **Icons, not words or real logos.** Heart, play triangle and game controller stand for
  Twitch follows, YouTube subscriptions and Steam ownership without brand marks, and they
  survive every locale. The speech bubble and chain link stand for the Discord post and
  the web link.
- **The two switches carry "on independently."** If the render drops one, the image
  becomes "a room with two doors" and loses the feature. Check them first.
- **Lime green as "on."** Ways in has no category hue of its own: the brand green on the
  switches doubles as the on state. Don't add another hue for it.
- **Inspect the guests.** They are the miniatures, and miniatures break first: hands,
  eyes, badge icons.
- **Google sign-in is not shown.** A fourth guest would crowd the doorway, and the post's
  angle is Twitch, YouTube and game owners.
