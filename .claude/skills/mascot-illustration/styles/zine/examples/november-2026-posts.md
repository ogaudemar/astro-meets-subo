# November 2026 posts: first Zine scene plates

The first three blog heroes in Zine. Rewritten 2026-11-12 as **scene plates** (system.md
v1.1, §7): ChatGPT draws the props and paper with a reserved zone, and the locked pose is
composited in the repo. Status: **briefed, not yet generated.** Record each result here
(approved, rejected and why) so the next brief learns from it.

Each block is pasted into a new chat of the "Subo Zine" project (setup and the plate rules
are in [../prompt-template.md](../prompt-template.md)). All positions are percentages of the
2:1 picture. Placements follow the convention table in the prompt template.

## 1. Upload Images and Vote on People, Games and Dates (live, needs its hero first)

Pose **P04** (kit v1.1). Output: `public/images/blog/images-vote/images-vote-hero.webp`.

```
PLATE FOR: blog post "Upload Images and Vote on People, Games and Dates in Discord"
SCENE: a poll card that now carries a real picture and real choices: a person, a game and a date.
SUBO (added later, do not draw): standing on the left, presenting with the right arm; hand at 34% across, 59% down; feet on a ground line at 88% down.
RESERVED ZONE: 6-37% across, 8-90% down, empty paper only.
MAIN PROP: an R04 poll card, black ink, from 39% to 86% across and 16% to 88% down, standing on the ground line, its left edge right next to Subo's hand as if he is holding it out. Across its top, a photo frame with a simple mountain-and-sun picture symbol. Below, three option rows, each marked only by an icon: a head-and-shoulders silhouette, a game controller, a blank calendar page. A tick in the controller row's box.
SUPPORT SYMBOLS: 1) an upload symbol (an arrow pointing up out of a tray) just right of the photo frame, at about 90% across, 22% down; 2) one small ink sparkle beside the ticked row, at about 89% across, 58% down.
TEXTURE: one soft halftone patch under the card on the ground line; nothing else.
NO TEXT: the calendar page is blank; no numbers, dates or letters anywhere.
```

## 2. File Uploads in Discord

**v1 (2026-11-13), replaced:** P04 presenting a speech bubble that files slide into. The
plate was good (the paperclip needed one redo, as "the standard attachment icon"), but side
by side with post 1 on the homepage the two read as the same picture: Subo presenting a big
object on the right. Lesson: check a new hero against the heroes it will sit next to.

**v2: the hoist.** Pose **P13 haul** (kit v1.1, proposed). Subo hauls a rope over a pulley
and lifts a crate of media up toward the conversation: uploading as upward effort. Output:
`public/images/blog/file-uploads/file-uploads-hero.webp`.

```
PLATE FOR: blog post "File Uploads in Discord: Collect Screenshots, Clips and PDFs"
SCENE: Subo hauls on a rope to lift a heavy crate of files up into the conversation.
SUBO (added later, do not draw): bottom left, leaning back and pulling, feet on a ground line at 95% down. He grips the rope at 21% across, 74% down and at 26% across, 66% down.
RESERVED ZONE: 4-30% across, 27-97% down, empty paper only, except the rope passing through the grip points.
ROPE: one thick black-ink rope in a straight line from 18% across, 79% down, through both grip points, rising to a pulley at 45% across, 35% down. Over the pulley it drops straight down on the pulley's right side to the crate. Keep it taut and straight.
MAIN PROP: a pulley wheel (black ink, with its hook) hanging from the underside of a large R02 speech bubble that spans 42-94% across and 4-30% down, with a small padlock drawn inside the bubble. A wooden-look crate, black ink, hanging from the rope at 48-70% across, 44-72% down, packed so the tops of three file cards stick out: an image card (mountain-and-sun), a document card with a folded corner and plain lines, a video card with a play triangle.
SUPPORT SYMBOLS: 1) two small ink sweat drops just right of Subo's head, at about 30% across, 26-32% down; 2) two or three short upward motion strokes beside the crate, at about 73% across, 50-65% down.
TEXTURE: one light halftone patch on the ground under the crate's shadow; nothing else.
NO TEXT: no file names, no extensions, no lines that look like words, nothing written on the crate.
```

## 3. Optional Questions

Pose **P05** (kit v1.1), centered and airborne. Output:
`public/images/blog/optional-questions/optional-questions-hero.webp`.

```
PLATE FOR: blog post "Optional Questions: Let Members Skip in Discord Surveys"
SCENE: Subo happily hops over one question it doesn't need to answer and lands on the next one.
SUBO (added later, do not draw): mid-jump, centered at 50% across, between 12% and 68% down; hands at 42% and 58% across, about 42% down.
RESERVED ZONE: 39-61% across, 9-71% down, empty paper only.
MAIN PROP: three square question cards, black ink, lying on a ground line at 86% down like stepping stones, each about 13% wide, centered at 20%, 50% and 80% across. The outer two show only a question-mark symbol; the middle one, directly under Subo, shows a skip-forward symbol (two triangles and a bar) instead. A tick on the first card's top corner.
SUPPORT SYMBOLS: 1) a dotted ink arc from the top of the first card up to the left edge of the reserved zone at about 35% down, and from the right edge of the zone at about 35% down to the top of the third card (the arc's middle is left out: Subo fills it); 2) a small ink burst of 3-4 short rays above the reserved zone, centered at 50% across, 3-8% down.
TEXTURE: none beyond the paper grain. No torn edges or tape.
NO TEXT: the question mark and the skip symbol are the only marks on the cards.
```

"?" is punctuation rather than a word, so it should pass the "universal symbols" rule. If it
reads as text, use a blank card with a single dot.
