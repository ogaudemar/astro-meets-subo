## Optional Questions

`/blog/discord-survey-optional-questions/` → `public/images/blog/optional-questions/optional-questions-hero.webp`

```
SCENE: Subo happily hops over one question it doesn't need to answer and lands on the next one.
POSE: P05 (jump / celebrate, mid-leap, body tilted forward, blocky legs tucked)
MAIN PROP: a row of three square question cards laid on the ground like stepping stones, each showing only a question-mark symbol. The middle card, the one Subo leaps over, shows a skip-forward symbol (two triangles and a bar) instead. Proposed prop R14 "skip card".
LAYOUT: L01 variant: cards run left to right along the bottom, Subo in the air above the middle card, a dotted ink arc tracing the jump from card one to card three
SUPPORT SYMBOLS: 1) a checkmark on the first card's corner (answered); 2) none other
DENSITY: website
OUTPUT: 2:1 landscape, natural off-white paper background, no torn edges or tape. Keep Subo and all three cards inside the central 1.91:1 area. The question mark and skip symbol are the only marks on the cards: no words, no numbers.
```

Open question on this one: "?" is punctuation rather than a word, so it should pass the
"universal symbols" rule. If it reads as text, use a blank card with a single dot.
