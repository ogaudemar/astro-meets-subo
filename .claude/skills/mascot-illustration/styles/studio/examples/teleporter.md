# Example: The Teleporter ("Subo Beams its Clones")

- **Feature:** Cloning / distributing a survey to another server.
- **Metaphor:** Teleporter (retro sci-fi).
- **Premise:** Subo teleports a clone of itself from the ship to a distant
  planet; the clone rematerializes fresh and clean, recolored to the planet's color to mark it as a copy which adapts to its new environment.

## Brief
Main Subo, dressed as a spaceship captain in a retro sci-fi uniform (no captain
hat, no insignia), stands at a cute retro sci-fi teleporter console and presses
the big button.

- **Left pad (inside the ship):** a blue-suited mini-Subo (slightly smaller,
  bar charts in a different color) actively dematerializing in sparkles.
- **Destination:** the right teleport pad is **not** inside the ship. It sits on
  the **purple planet** visible through the window. A **purple-suited** mini-Subo
  rematerializes there, slightly glowing, fresh and clean.
- **Clone recolor:** the planet clone has **all-purple bar charts with pink
  outlines**, and is **wide-eyed with both eyes in the left-eye style** (square
  with inner square) as a newly arrived copy.
- **Queue:** two more mini-Subos wait behind the left pad in different colored
  uniforms (e.g. red and gold), nodding at each other.
- **Console text:** "TELEPORT CLONE".
- **Interior palette:** warm metallic gray, teal accents, pink highlights; much
  less purple inside the ship. Purple is reserved for the destination planet.
- **Uniforms:** Star-Trek-reminiscent but **no Starfleet insignias** on any
  uniform.
- **Composition:** simple, uncluttered, clear focus on the teleportation action.
- **Ratio:** 2:1.

## Notes
- Canonical source of two rules: **clone = sparkle**, and **clones recolor bar
  charts + flip both eyes to the left-eye style.**
- Purple-as-destination is the canonical "purple only where intended" example.
