# Example: The Live Poll Desk

- **Feature:** Polls / voting (hero image for the "Best Discord poll bots" post).
- **Metaphor:** Broadcast "poll desk" (election-night results show).
- **Premise:** Subo cheerfully hosts a live poll he's clearly winning. The gag is
  that the poll is about *how* people vote, not about competitors, so it's
  self-deprecating rather than a swipe at named rivals.

## Brief
Subo as a warm TV broadcast host (light blazer, small microphone, big grin) at a
soft-metallic desk in a cozy studio, gesturing toward a big glowing live-results
board. Looking toward camera, presenting.

- **The board (the joke):** title "HOW DO WE VOTE?" with three horizontal result
  bars, tallest to shortest: **Subo** (tall teal/cyan bar, winning, still climbing),
  **Thumbs in chat** (short), **A spreadsheet** (shortest, sad little bar). A small
  "LIVE" on-air light nearby.
- **Foreground:** one Discord blob voting on a floating, glowing Discord poll embed
  card that mirrors the board's question; wide-eyed, delighted "aha" as its choice
  lights up. A tiny "+XP" chip pops onto the blob as a thank-you for voting (single
  small reward accent, not a factory).
- **Tone:** playful, warm.
- **Ratio:** 2:1.

## Notes
- Keep the poll about the **method of voting**, never named competitors: naming
  rivals in a poll Subo wins undercuts an honest-comparison article.
- Winner bar in **teal/cyan** (Subo's aqua) so it reads as the winner. Purple omitted.
- Distinct from the game-show stage (`scoring-quiz`, quizzes) and the control room
  (monitoring): warm TV studio, a single poll with option bars, not scoreboards or
  neon screens.
- Expect heavy text cleanup in Photopea: the render tends to duplicate labels (e.g.
  "Thumbs in chat 👍/👎 in chat") and scatter extra "SUBO" branding (mic, mug, an
  invented desk banner). Emoji render messy; redraw or retype.
