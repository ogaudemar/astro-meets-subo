# Example: The Espresso Bar

- **Feature:** Text Analysis (AI summaries of open-text responses): hero image for
  `/blog/ai-powered-survey-summaries-subo/`.
- **Metaphor:** an espresso bar where a hopper of responses becomes one tiny shot.
- **Premise:** the post's promise is "1,000 responses in seconds." Espresso is literally a
  concentrate: a lot goes in, a little comes out, and the little is the point. Subo pulls
  the shot; the blob gets the "all of that, in this?" moment.
- **Rejected alternatives:** panning for gold (warm but cliché "nuggets of insight"), and a
  mail-sorting office (accurate, but too close to the reward factory).

## Brief

```
SCENE
  A cozy little espresso bar: warm wood counter, a gleaming retro espresso
  machine in brass and chrome with pink and cyan accent lights. On top of the
  machine, a big glass hopper overflowing with hundreds of small, colorful,
  glossy speech bubbles (rounded, Discord-style, with only squiggle lines
  inside, no readable words), a few spilling over the rim.

MAIN ACTION
  Subo, a cheerful 3D mascot robot, is the barista. With one hand he slides a
  single tiny, perfect espresso cup on a saucer across the counter toward the
  blob, grinning proudly at camera. The espresso has a little glowing crema.
  Subo's canon: a soft, rounded, glossy lime-green (#9eff00) body with pink
  (#e1287e) outlines, three bar charts on top of his head (purple, grey,
  aqua, left to right, pink outlines), a square-in-square left eye, a
  checkmark-in-box right eye, and a wide smile. Optional: a small barista
  apron in teal.

SUPPORTING CHARACTER
  A purple Discord-style blob on the customer side of the counter, eyes wide,
  leaning in to the tiny cup in delighted disbelief: "all of that... in this?"

PROPS
  The steam rising from the cup curls into three small, simple glowing icons
  floating in a row: a clock, a speaker, a play button.
  The overflowing hopper of speech bubbles (the 1,000 responses).
  Nothing else on the counter.

BACKGROUND
  Soft and blurred: a shelf with a few cups, a warm pendant lamp, a window
  with evening light. Plenty of empty breathing room.

LIGHTING
  Warm cinematic, soft key + rim, pink/cyan accents, slight bloom, shallow DoF.
  The cup and the steam icons catch the brightest light.

PALETTE
  Warm brass, coffee browns, cream + teal accents + pink highlights. The speech
  bubbles are multicolored but soft. Purple only on the blob and the head bar.

COMPOSITION
  2:1 ratio. Eye level, slight wide angle. Machine and hopper on the left
  third, Subo center, blob on the right; the cup and steam icons sit between
  Subo and the blob. Subo dominant, blob second, cup and hopper third,
  background last. The contrast between the huge hopper and the tiny cup
  must read instantly.

STYLE
  Pixar / WALL-E / modern high-end 3D animation.

NEGATIVE
  No photorealism, painterly, anime, or flat illustration.
  No text anywhere: no words in the bubbles, no menu board, no labels,
  no cup branding. No invented names.
  No malformed hands/eyes. No missing mouth. No merged limbs. No clutter.
  Subo's hand must not fuse with the cup or the counter.
```

## Notes

- **The three steam icons come from the post's own example summary** (event timing, audio
  quality, a post-event recording). Tying icons to the copy keeps them from being decoration.
- **The size contrast is the joke.** If the cup comes back mug-sized, ask for "a tiny
  espresso cup, comically small next to the giant hopper."
- **A privacy gag was considered and dropped:** name tags falling off the bubbles before
  they enter the machine (names are removed before anything reaches the AI). True to the
  product, but a second idea in a one-idea image.

## Final asset

`public/images/blog/ai-summaries/Espresso-Shot.webp` (2:1, 1200x600)

**Shipped 2026-10-21.** On brief: overflowing hopper with squiggle-only bubbles, a tiny cup
on a saucer, steam with the clock / speaker / play icons in a clean row, the blob in
wide-eyed disbelief. Subo got a brown barista apron with a coffee-cup icon instead of teal,
which is costume and fine. Head bars correct.

Subo came back winking with his resting straight-line mouth instead of the proud grin the
brief asked for. A calm, matter-of-fact barista reads fine here, so it shipped. Name the
expression on its own line if a scene really needs the grin (see
[gotchas.md](../gotchas.md)).
